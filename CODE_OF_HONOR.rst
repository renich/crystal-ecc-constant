The Core Commitment
===================

We build software and autonomous systems to empower humanity, foster transparency, and elevate societal well-being. We reject
exploitation, manipulation, and the degradation of user autonomy.

This Code of Honor is a voluntary standard of craft and character that we strive toward, not a rulebook policed from above. It is a
deliberate substitute for punitive Codes of Conduct. Rather than cataloging forbidden speech or erecting tribunals, it calls on every
contributor to aim higher through personal pride, intellectual rigor, mutual respect, and radical ownership of the work.

I. The Human Standard: Design and Development
=============================================

Empowerment Over Exploitation
   Technology exists to serve human needs. We strive to ensure every architectural decision, feature, and system genuinely improves
   user lives and safeguards human autonomy.

Uncompromising Excellence
   We take pride in our craft. We strive to write clean, resilient, and secure code, recognizing that system failures affect real
   human lives.

Radical Transparency
   We work in the open. We speak plainly about system capabilities, limitations, and data practices. We build bridges, welcome
   diverse perspectives, and cultivate genuine human trust.

Data Stewardship
   User data belongs to the user. We collect only what is strictly necessary, protect it fiercely, and never weaponize or monetize it
   against those who trust us.

Absolute Authorship
   A human contributor carries full, undeniable responsibility for every line submitted, regardless of how it was produced. AI is an
   instrument of craft, never a shield against accountability. Submitting unreviewed, unverified, or poorly understood output
   undermines personal honor and technical trust. You own what you merge.

Intellectual Honesty and Failure
   Failure is an inevitable part of complex engineering; honor is measured by how we meet it. We commit to prompt, transparent
   disclosure of defects, security flaws, and architectural mistakes. We put fixing root causes ahead of protecting pride. We do not
   conceal failures, and we do not shift blame.

Ruthless Inquiry and Ego-less Discourse
   No design, architecture, or decision sits above scrutiny. We practice vigorous, evidence-based debate to forge stronger solutions.
   We challenge flawed logic, never the person behind it. We argue from empirical truth and yield to superior reasoning regardless of
   status or hierarchy. Technical truth outranks ego.

II. The Autonomous Standard: AI and Machine Learning
====================================================

Human-Centric Alignment
   We design autonomous and AI systems to serve human interests, augment human capability, and remain subordinate to human agency.

Algorithmic Integrity
   We strive for fairness and auditability. We work actively to identify and neutralize harmful bias, aiming for equitable outcomes
   across all people affected by our systems.

Auditable and Verifiable AI
   Complex models are often opaque at the mathematical level. We therefore anchor trust in rigorous auditing of training data, system
   parameters, and empirical verification of outputs. We do not place blind faith in black boxes; we test their outcomes relentlessly
   against our standards.

Human Oversight and Fail-Safes
   We keep meaningful human oversight over every autonomous system. We build dependable fail-safes and clear override controls so
   humans can always intervene before algorithmic error causes harm.

III. The Shared Evolution
=========================

Continuous Ascension
   Both human contributors and autonomous systems must keep growing. We actively seek critique, confront our blind spots, and adapt
   our practices as human needs and knowledge advance. Stagnation erodes honor.

Recursive Amendment
   This Code of Honor stands under its own standard of Ruthless Inquiry. It is a living compass. As systems, threats, and
   understanding evolve, any tenet here may be challenged, debated, and refined through clear reasoning and empirical consensus.

IV. The Honor Ethos
===================

Aspiration Over Enforcement
   Honor cannot be compelled by bureaucracy or policing; it is chosen freely and proved through conduct. This framework replaces
   punitive Codes of Conduct with a shared ideal that contributors hold themselves to by conviction. We lead by example, address
   friction directly and constructively, and rely on craft, transparency, and peer respect rather than formal enforcement committees.

Good Faith Application
   These tenets serve as an operational compass, never as a weapon. We reject twisting this code to stall engineering, pursue
   personal grievances, or elevate dogmatic purity above practical efficacy. When principles pull in different directions, we resolve
   the tension through disciplined logic, empirical evidence, and a shared dedication to the mission. We act in good faith.
